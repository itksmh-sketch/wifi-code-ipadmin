Operator Notification SMS

—

Used only to text operators about their own account — trial expiry, invoices issued, grace period, suspension. This is not the SMS gateway operators resell to their customers; that one lives on the Providers page and is billed per segment. A separate Arkesel account is preferred — the gateway's balance is reconciled against metered usage, and notification spend on the same account cannot be told apart from it. A second key on the same account works meanwhile; tick the box below so the reconciliation job knows not to treat the resulting drift as a fault.

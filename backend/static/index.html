Features

Everything a hotspot business needs

Your routers, plans, payments and people in one place. Your customers only need a phone and a browser.

Routers over a secure VPN

A guided four-step setup connects each MikroTik router over its own WireGuard tunnel, even behind NAT. You can see health and active users, and reboot from the dashboard.

Flexible plans and vouchers

Sell time-based, data-based or hybrid plans with their own speed limits. Generate voucher batches and print them for walk-in sales.

Mobile money, straight to you

Customers pick a plan on your hotspot login page and pay with mobile money. The money goes to your own Paystack or Flutterwave account, and the voucher works straight away.

Your brand on the login page

The captive portal shows your name, logo, welcome message and contact details. A live phone preview shows exactly what customers will see.

Vouchers by SMS

Buyers get their voucher code by text message. Use your own Arkesel or Hubtel account, or send through the platform's gateway.

Your own resellers Coming soon

Add your own resellers and give them codes to sell for you. You set their commission, see every code they sell, and record the money they hand over.

Live sessions and analytics

See who is online right now, revenue and redemptions for the last 30 days, your top package and which routers are offline.

Team roles and security

Add admin and staff users with the right permissions. A security PIN protects sensitive changes, and an audit log records who did what.

Fair time, full control

A voucher's clock starts at first sign-in and keeps counting across reconnects. Disable a lost or stolen voucher and anyone using it is cut off straight away.

How it works

From application to first sale

Four steps, all from your dashboard.

  1. Apply

    Tell us about your business. We review every application, usually within 1–2 business days, and send your sign-in details.

  2. Connect a router

    Paste the generated commands into your MikroTik to bring up the VPN tunnel. The wizard then sets up the hotspot for you.

  3. Create plans and connect payments

    Set your prices and add your Paystack or Flutterwave keys to take mobile money.

  4. Start selling

    Customers buy on the login page, or you print vouchers for cash sales. Soon you'll also give codes to your own resellers to sell for you.

Questions

Frequently asked

What equipment do I need?

A MikroTik router running RouterOS 7, which includes WireGuard, and an internet connection. Your router doesn't need a public IP address, because it connects out to us over the VPN.

Do my customers need an app?

No. When they join your Wi-Fi, a login page opens in their browser. They can enter a voucher code or buy a plan with mobile money right there.

Where does the money from sales go?

To your own Paystack or Flutterwave account. You enter your own keys, so customer payments never pass through us. We only bill you the monthly fee.

Can I run more than one location?

Yes. Organise your network into towns and sites and add as many routers as you need. The monthly fee stays the same.

What happens when the free trial ends?

You get a monthly invoice in your dashboard's Billing page and can pay it there by mobile money or card.
